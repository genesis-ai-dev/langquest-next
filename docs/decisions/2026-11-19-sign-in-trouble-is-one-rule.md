# One rule in the database says which sign-in records are trouble, and each kind of trouble is a line on the ops dashboard's Overview

Date: 2026-11-19 · By: Caleb Koster · Status: accepted

Reason: on 19 November 2026 Caleb saw a chart of many sign-in problems on the ops dashboard's "Phones and browsers" page and asked why the Overview reported none. Two rules read the same `auth` records. The chart "Sign-in trouble", its table and the records behind a point counted every step that did not end `ok`, `done`, `keep` or `home` (`diag.daily_compute`, `diag.ops_clients`, `diag.detail_filters`): also a step with no connection, a wrong password, an invite that expired and a new person with no organization yet. The line (`clientAlerts` in `apps/admin/src/sources/diag.ts`) read only a step that ended `server`, `failed` or `unreachable` on three or more installs in a week. So the chart called records trouble that are none, and trouble on one or two phones never made a line. A status shows only what is verified, and a problem a section shows must show on the Overview. So one function, `diag.sign_in_class` (migration `20261119171500_sign_in_trouble.sql`), gives each record a class, and everything reads it. `ok` is a step that went as designed, counted nowhere: `ok`, `done`, `keep`, `home`, and a new person asked for a name or with no organization yet (`needs_name`, `no_org`). `no_fault` is a step that did not finish where neither the server nor the app failed: no connection (`offline`, `unreachable`), a change the app tries again (`retry`), a first screen before the organization was known (`org_unknown`; the read that failed has its own record), an entry the server refused (`refused` on `sign_in`, `sign_up`, `code`, `v2`: a wrong password or code, an email that has an account) and an invite that cannot be used (`expired`, `used`, `not_found`, `retired`). `trouble` is all the rest: `server`, `failed`, `gone`, `http`, `rate_limited`, a sign-in that ended without the person ending it (`ended`), a refusal on a step that holds no entry of a person, and any outcome the rule does not name, so a new outcome shows until someone says what it is (`scripts/diagSchema.test.ts` makes the developer who adds one say). "Sign-in trouble" counts trouble only, and each kind of it in the week is a line, on one phone too, so the chart and the Overview cannot disagree. A second number, "Sign-in steps refused or with no connection", counts `no_fault` and makes no line, so no record leaves the page (as a first download's wait stayed in view, `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-first-download-time-without-the-wait.md`). The watch for user issues (decisions.md 202) is unchanged: it reads every record here but `offline`, and Claude judges each new kind. Rejected: a line for every record the old chart counted (wrong passwords and phones with no connection would hold a line for good at any fleet size); leaving `no_fault` records out of the page; a rule kept in the page's code beside the SQL (two copies made this defect); and keeping the limit of three installs (the fleet is tens of phones, and with it the chart shows trouble the Overview does not).
Reverse if: lines of sign-in trouble on one phone stay for weeks without a cause to fix as the fleet grows (then give the line a share of the phones that reported, and say that limit at the chart), or production records show a class holds the wrong outcomes (move the outcome in `diag.sign_in_class`; the days that still have raw records are counted again by a migration, as this one does).
