# The help page links a screen to its tutorial videos by the app's language and build, and shows nothing when none fits

Date: 2026-11-17 · By: Caleb Koster · Status: accepted

Reason: LAN-244 asks for each screen's tutorial video and download on the help page, matched to the app's version (store build and update) and its language. When no video fits, Caleb chose to hide the link: no English stand-in, no list, no warning. A wrong-language or wrong-version video could show buttons the person does not have. Most screens are in several videos, so the help page lists every video that spends two or more steps on the screen (one step is most often the way through), the one with the most steps first (Caleb, 2026-11-19: "Every video, best first"); `e2e/tutorials/screen-videos.ts` writes that table from the videos' steps into `packages/core/src/tutorialScreens.ts`. `packages/core/src/tutorialLinks.ts` picks (`pickTutorialLinks`: screen to videos, same language only, left out when filmed for another store build or a newer update than the phone's); the Worker lists a language's chosen videos at `/api/tutorial-links` (only for a language with its own approved catalog, so an unsupported one is not answered in English); help mode's play button opens the list: a tap on a video watches it on YouTube, its disc downloads it, using the lasting links of decision 187. Considered and turned down: English stand-in, the full videos list, the closest video with a warning, one video for each screen. The build fields (`runtimes`, `filmedFrom`) are read when the list carries them; the filming does not record them yet, so until it does only language decides, which is true of the web app always.
Reverse if: people in languages with few videos ask where the video is; then show the English one, labelled.
