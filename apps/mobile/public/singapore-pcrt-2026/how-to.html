Phase 1 · Prepare

Before you start

  • Choose two or three languages.
  • Name a coordinator for each language.
  • Agree on source material, roles, and review steps.
  • Plan devices, connectivity, and training.
  • Agree on consent and who may hear the work.
  • Decide how the team asks for help.

Phones: use trusted devices from reputable sellers. A counterfeit phone can report storage it does not have, and recordings can be lost.

Phase 2 · Set up · 1 of 5

Set up your organization in five steps.

A short checklist guides the admin: name the organization, add a language, choose how passages are checked, decide who does what, and invite the team.

Phase 2 · Set up · 2 of 5

Add a language. Choose what to translate.

Each language gets a name, a scope (New Testament, Old Testament, or the whole Bible), and a template that divides the text into passages.

Phase 2 · Set up · 3 of 5

Give translators what they learn from.

Reference material lives at the organization level: key terms, study guides, and the questions reviewers ask. Follow what others share, copy it, or publish your own.

Phase 2 · Set up · 4 of 5

Decide how passages are checked.

Pick a review flow, or build your own. Here, each passage goes through community check, peer review, back translation, consultant check, and a local check.

Phase 2 · Set up · 5 of 5

Decide who can do what.

Roles are sets of permissions. Start with the standard roles: admin, coordinator, translator, reviewer, and viewer. Switch any permission on or off.

Set up once. Adjust as you learn.

Phase 3 · Invite

Invite people with a QR code.

Choose a role and add a name. The app shows a QR code. People without email scan it to join. The code works once and expires.

The code can also be typed or sent as a link.

Phase 3 · Invite

Joining takes one scan.

The new member scans the code and joins. The welcome screen says who invited them and what their role is.

Phase 4 · Record

A translator's first minutes.

My Work shows what is next. The Map shows every passage in the language and how far each has come. Anyone on the team can start a passage.

Phase 4 · Record

Record a passage, even offline.

Open a passage and tap record. Listen back. Record another version when needed. The phone keeps the work and syncs when it connects.

Phase 4 · Record

Ask someone to record a passage.

Pick a person. Add directions by voice or text. Set a date if needed. The request appears on their My Work and Inbox.

Phase 5 · Review

Review early, one step at a time.

Each passage shows which review steps it has cleared. Tap a step to ask someone, record that it already happened, or set it aside with a reason.

The reason stays in the history. Anyone can see why a step was skipped.

When conditions change

What if…

No signal for weeks?Keep recording on the phone. It syncs when the connection returns.
Reviewers disagree?Follow the decision rule your organization agreed in advance.
A team member leaves?Assignments, notes, and recordings stay visible to the rest of the team.
A phone is lost?Synced work is safe. Work that never synced is lost, so sync when you can.
A problem appears late?Correct it, and keep the reason with the work.

Questions · Trust and control

Trust and control

← All questions
Who owns the recordings?Your organization does. Work starts as “All rights reserved.” You can open it later, never close it again.
Who can see our work?Only members of your organization, unless you make it public. Each role decides what a person can do.
Can someone change what they should not?Roles and permissions control that. Moving a passage past a review step is its own permission, and the reason is logged.
Can we delete our data or leave?Yes. A person can ask for their data to be removed, and the organization is told.

Questions · Offline and devices

Offline and devices

← All questions
Does it work without internet?Yes. Recording, reviewing, and finding your next task all work offline. Some licensed reference Bibles need a connection.
Two people on one passage, offline?Each phone keeps its own record. They merge when they sync. Nothing is overwritten.
A phone is lost before it syncs?Work that never synced lives only on that phone, so it is lost. Sync when you can.
Which phones?iPhone or Android. Not top-tier, but trusted, from a reputable seller.

Questions · Quality and review

Quality and review

← All questions
When is a passage good enough?When it has cleared the review steps your organization chose. Each passage shows where it stands.
Feedback from people without the app?Share clips on WhatsApp. The person who hears the feedback records it on the passage. A review link is coming.
Can we assign work?Yes. Pick a person, add directions by voice or text, and set a date. It appears on their My Work and Inbox.

Questions · Getting started

Getting started

← All questions
How long is setup?A five-step checklist. New members see who invited them, their role, and a short getting-started card.
No email?Make a QR code in the app. They scan it and join. It works once and expires.
Which languages?Every language. LangQuest is audio first, so a language does not need a written form.
How do we get the audio out?Export from the website, or connect through the API.
